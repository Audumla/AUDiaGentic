# Step 4 cannot work as written — SessionLifecycleDecision carries no correlation context

## Notes

External adversarial review 2026-08-09, claim verified directly against the tree. AS93 step 4 says it accepts already-normalized lifecycle 'evidence and decisions' and emits events carrying sequence, timestamp, session id, turn id and correlation id. Verified in components/agents/status/session_lifecycle_projection.py: SessionLifecycleDecision has exactly coarse_state, accepts_new_turn, session_reusable, turn_terminal, dependent_work_releasable, evidence_state, reason — none of session id, turn id, sequence, timestamp or correlation id. A bare decision is therefore not a viable event source. SessionEvidenceProjection.accept() is the seam that does hold correlated StatusEvidence and builds SessionLifecycleEvidence with those fields before deriving the decision.

Second finding: AS93's 'cursor integrity ... excluded' wording is now too broad given RV921 moved structural cursor validation INTO this item. Third: AS93 still says caller authorization is excluded and owned by hardening items — stale, no such item exists after the AS101 rewrite. Fourth: reviewer questions whether AS93 needs its own public MCP read tool (step 7) at all, since AS94 immediately supersedes it with the real public live-turn read — a standalone metadata-only surface adds another public contract and another AS102 allowlist obligation with no demonstrated consumer.

## Findings



## Conclusion

Four edits needed. (1) Rewrite step 4 to tap the SessionEvidenceProjection.accept() seam and persist already-correlated lifecycle evidence, never reconstructing context from a bare SessionLifecycleDecision. (2) Change the integrity exclusion to 'cryptographic signing/tamper integrity excluded; structural parsing, bounds and cursor-context validation in scope'. (3) Delete the stale caller-authorization deferral. (4) Keep the RV921 cursor outcomes semantically DISTINCT rather than collapsed: malformed/oversized -> registered bad-input error; wrong session -> registered context-mismatch error; old generation after restart -> deterministic gap; expired sequence -> deterministic retention gap. Consider demoting step 7's MCP surface to an internal/application read primitive unless a standalone metadata consumer is proven.
