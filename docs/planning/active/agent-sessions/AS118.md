# Authoritative session mutation fence for resume and purge

## Description

Add the missing per-session lifecycle fence required to serialize explicit/automatic resume and existing-session turn admission against destructive gateway session-lineage purge. Extend durable session records with a retention claim and an authoritative accepted-turn timestamp without introducing a retired lifecycle state.

## Steps

1. Add a generation.lock path and session_generation_fence() separate from mutation.lock; document one lock ordering.
2. Add durable retention-claim fields and guarded claim/release/delete helpers under the authoritative fence.
3. Persist last-accepted-turn-at at the durable turn-admission point, not completion or provider activity.
4. Hold the fence across source reread, resume eligibility, claim check, provider resume/open, successor creation/linking, and idempotency commit; hold it only through durable acceptance for ordinary new-turn admission.
5. Reject resume/admission when a conflicting retention claim exists and make crash/replay behavior deterministic.
6. Add race, crash, deadlock, and accepted-turn timestamp tests across explicit resume, automatic continuation, and existing-session admission.

## Detailed Solution & Technical Design

AS108 owns only the authoritative session mutation fence and the durable facts needed by SH32. Use a per-session generation.lock in addition to the existing mutation.lock; never hold mutation.lock across provider I/O. Lock ordering is generation.lock -> mutation.lock and never the inverse. The durable retention claim survives process death because a filesystem lock does not.

The claim is a bounded record field such as retention-claim: {operation-id, claimed-at}; it is not a new session lifecycle state. claim_retention, release_retention_claim (before destructive work only), and delete_claimed_session must verify the expected terminal facts, current generation/revision, and matching operation id.

Resume/admit-new-turn and session purge must commit against the same generation fence. Explicit resume holds the source fence across reread, eligibility validation, retention-claim check, provider resume/open, successor creation/linking, and idempotency result. Normal turn admission holds it only until the turn is durably accepted/queued. Persist last-accepted-turn-at at that acceptance point; do not substitute close time, completion time, polling, watchdog, or provider activity. AS108 changes no retention policy or deletion behavior.

## Validation

Resume wins a purge race and records accepted activity; purge then rereads as ineligible. Purge wins first and explicit/automatic resume performs no provider resume call. Existing-session admission cannot use stale inactivity after an accepted turn. A crash after retention claim prevents resume and allows deterministic replay. Provider metadata/checkpoint paths using mutation.lock do not deadlock while generation.lock is held. Existing AS107 resume/relocation and SH24 request-purge tests remain unchanged.

## Effort & Risk

Medium/high concurrency risk. The critical hazards are lock inversion, holding mutation.lock across provider I/O, stale accepted-turn timestamps, and claims that do not survive process death.
