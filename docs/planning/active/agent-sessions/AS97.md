# Prove OpenCode ACP permission, tool, cancel and rollback behaviour

## Description

Close the behavioural proof gaps for the OpenCode ACP surface. The transport works and is validated — `opencode-acp` is marked validated in the observability inventory with a real e2e anchor, and `build_acp_launch` has construction-level unit coverage. What that proves is a plain prompt round-trip and nothing beyond it.

Five specific behaviours have no coverage anywhere in the tree — confirmed by direct search of both the unit and e2e suites.

## Steps

1. Permission request and response during a real turn.
2. Tool-call stimulus and the resulting event and status shape.
3. Cancellation semantics — specifically distinguishing acknowledgement from terminal/idle evidence.
4. Session binding persistence rollback on a failed open: prove a failed bind closes the provisional owned transport and leaves no active record or index entry.
5. Content-channel-disabled verification: prove assistant text does not reach status, timeline or lifecycle evidence while the surface's content channel stays unenabled.
6. Update the surface declaration to exactly what these probes prove — no more.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All five previously-unproven behaviours have direct test coverage: permission, tool call, cancellation semantics, binding rollback on failed open, and content-channel-disabled verification.
- Cancellation acknowledgement is proven distinct from terminal/idle evidence.
- A failed bind leaves no active record, index entry or orphaned transport.
- Assistant text is proven absent from status, timeline and lifecycle evidence while the content channel stays unenabled.
- Every capability promoted in the surface declaration traces to one of these probes; nothing is claimed that the probes did not exercise.
- OpenCode and ACP details stay provider-private.
