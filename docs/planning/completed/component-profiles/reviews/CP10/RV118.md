## Notes



## Findings

What landed: foundation/registry_utils.py (Registry with alias support) and its adoption inside foundation/components/registry.py (which was CP08's scope). What did NOT land, despite the item being marked completed: (1) the constructor-supplied loader: Callable[[], None] lazy-load support — the headline feature, and the one ARCHITECTURE_STANDARDS.md §11 and the §12 anti-pattern row ('Hand-rolled _loaded/_ensure_loaded() guard per registry → Shared registry utility's built-in lazy-loader') now advertise; the standard points at a capability that does not exist. (2) ZERO of the five registry migrations: components/coding_lsp/language_registry.py, components/providers/tags/registry.py, components/providers/surfaces/registry.py (the two-dicts-one-loader case), foundation/toolchains/provision_steps/base.py, and host_adapter.py (which received only CP01's path fix) are all untouched — git status confirms none modified. (3) No caller updates, no old-function deletions — the item's own validation ('grep confirms zero remaining references to the deleted old register_*/get_* function names') is unsatisfiable. (4) Registry raises raw ValueError on collisions — the item's step 1 explicitly required a catalogued error modeled on capabilities.py (which raised AudiaGenticError VAL-CAP-001), and §8 forbids raw ValueError at public boundaries. (5) registry_utils.pop()'s docstring says it 'Exists for backwards compatibility with tests' — shim language contra §10.

## Conclusion

Reopen or re-scope honestly: either implement the loader= support and the five migrations (the item's actual substance), or split what shipped into a smaller completed item and restore the remainder as pending. Independently: convert ValueError to catalogued AudiaGenticError codes, and remove the backward-compat framing on pop(). Until loader= exists, §11/§12's references to it are documentation for vaporware — either build it or note the pending state.
