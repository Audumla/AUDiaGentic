# Review AR02: define capability registration owner and collision rule

## Notes



## Findings

The shared `foundation/capabilities.py` seam is stronger than three bespoke registries, but the plan still leaves two operational rules implicit: who owns registration timing, and what happens on duplicate key registration. Since AR19 may move lifecycle bootstraps into foundation, specify whether providers registers capabilities during descriptor load, component bootstrap, or observer subscription setup, and add a hard duplicate-key policy (`make_error` or idempotent same-fn only). Without that, early imports can silently win or mask ordering bugs.

## Conclusion

Good refactor shape; capability lifecycle and duplicate-key semantics should be explicit before implementation.
