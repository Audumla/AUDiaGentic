# Sequencing shape: 'open early, close late' rather than a small gating core

## Notes

Two-turn adversarial review, 2026-08-09. Opening position was 'AS102 must not gate the tranche, but every new projection obeys it from day one'. Reviewer initially proposed a small 'AS102-core' slice to land first, then retracted that after being challenged: AS102's own effort_risk names the reachability/conformance guard (proving no surface can serialize around the enforcement point) as the hard part, and that guard is precisely what the 'core' slice contained. Calling it small was wrong. Separately confirmed the shared redaction primitives (redact_text, structural denylist, safe_metadata allowlist) already exist and are consumed at the MCP boundary — AS102's addition is architectural (forcing all public session projections through one allowlisted mechanism), not primitive-building.

## Findings



## Conclusion

Do not split AS102 or describe an early slice as small/cheap; keep it at M as one item. Make its shape explicit: purely internal mechanics (AS93 buffer, AS87 internal output reader) may proceed without it; the central enforcement point plus its reachability guard must land with or before the FIRST new public projection; each later surface registers its allowlist through that mechanism; AS102 closes after the final cross-surface audit. Separate by when dependencies exist, not by effort.
