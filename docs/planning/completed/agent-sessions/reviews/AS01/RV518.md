# Overseer R&D transport findings: event-cap pressure on long runs; in-flight bump unsafe

## Notes

From .audiagentic/research/agent-overseer/README.md (live MA28 delegation trial, 2026-07-17). Two findings against the session transport as shipped in AS01: (1) long-running worker tasks can exhaust the per-turn bounded-delivery budgets (MAX_EVENTS 10k / 8MiB) BEFORE terminal completion — progress/terminal signal quality degrades exactly when a controller most needs it; (2) 'bumping' a stalled in-flight prompt is unsafe — cancelling the awaited prompt() marks the transport dead by design, so live continuation of a wedged turn requires a protocol-level mechanism, not caller-side cancellation. Same-session continuation AFTER a terminal turn is verified working.

## Findings

Incorporation: (a) AS06 gains a long-run budget test (fake agent emitting past MAX_EVENTS; assert terminal result integrity and dropped-event accounting) and evaluation of a checkpoint/compaction strategy or heartbeat-class events bypassing caps (any cap change is an MA18 review — budgets are frozen contract); (b) AS09's design must include the safe in-flight interaction primitive (protocol session/cancel then continuation, or turn/steer-style injection where the provider supports it — AS14 for codex) instead of transport-kill bumps. Facts recorded on opencode acp-live-session capability entry (constraints/limitations).

## Conclusion

Findings incorporated into AS06/AS09 scope and the opencode ACP capability facts; no immediate transport change (budgets are frozen per MA18; changes go through that gate). Closing.
