## Findings

1. **Depends on AS17 (ManagedProcess)** — Orphan detection relies on ManagedProcess lifecycle state from PR05/AS17. Without it, we'd be back to ad-hoc PID checks.

2. **Recovery timeout is necessary** — The force-close after N seconds prevents deadlocks when the transport doesn't acknowledge closing.

3. **External process distinction is correct** — Only reclaim slots for owned processes; external processes (AS14 App Server) are not our responsibility.
