## Findings

A stable scalar interleaving would require a new shared ordering authority or store (violates no-third-store) or an unstated cross-source fence/clock contract. 2. **One canonical façade, two source-native lanes — KEEP.** AS94's strongest legitimate purpose is preventing protocol adapters from reaching into transports, output files or event stores directly. Return `status_page` + `output_page` (or a vector-cursor response) from one side-effect-free read operation. **RESIZE** away from "one globally ordered stream".

3. **Outer cursor as opaque/versioned vector — RESIZE.** Carry AS93's next position/generation and AS87's next ordinal plus request/session/turn binding. Drop aggregate outer `gap`/`truncated` — those facts have different meanings and different authorities on each lane; keep them on the owning channel. `has_more` may remain as a mechanical `status.has_more OR output.has_more`.

4. **Delivery state in the envelope — DROP.** If it means acknowledged/watermark state, reconnect semantics require mutation and consumer-specific persistence, contradicting the pure-read contract. If it only derives availability from the two reads, it is a misnamed derived field. Remove it.

5. **Reconnect semantics — RESIZE.** Per-source continuation positions suffice to prevent source-level redelivery; the outer cursor packages them and binds them to request/session/turn but must not assign domain ordering. No cross-source watermark or dedup authority.

6. **Protocol-adapter boundary — KEEP.** Adaptors consuming the canonical live-read surface rather than transports, relay files or event stores is the right boundary and the defensible core of this item.

7. **Fold AS93's private metadata-read primitive into AS94 — FOLD/RESIZE.** If no separate metadata-only consumer emerges, AS94 becomes the canonical live-read façade over a metadata-read capability (AS93) and the output-read capability (AS87); the plan-item symmetry is then incidental, not structural.

## Conclusion

RESIZE AS94 substantially, do not drop. Thin version: a side-effect-free canonical live-read façade exposing two independently authoritative source-native reads (status and output), a vector cursor packaging their continuation positions plus context binding, gap facts owned by their sources, no global sequence, no delivery state, no third store. Fold AS93's primitive in if no independent consumer appears.
