# Evidence cardinality and per-list-member evidence are unmodeled

## Notes

Two structural mismatches between today's capability_facts and PC02's 'one Capability record per kind, with one optional evidence':

1. Multiple facts per subject. The current validator (capability_facts.py) only forbids duplicate capability_id WITHIN a provider — but nothing stops two DIFFERENT facts describing the same descriptor field (e.g. a verified 'mcp-config' fact plus a separate constraint/limitation fact). More importantly, evidence today carries its own constraints/limitations/action_needed/support_assessment/tool_version PER FACT. Collapsing to a single `evidence` object per kind loses the ability to record more than one evidence claim about one mechanism (e.g. pi has BOTH 'vendor-key-injection' AND 'model-config-surface' execution-tier facts, each with distinct constraints and the same subject family).

2. Per-member evidence for list kinds. Subjects today can target a specific list member: `host_capabilities[google.gemini-cli-vscode-ide-companion]`. Under a list-cardinality kind (host-extension), evidence must attach to a SPECIFIC list entry, not the whole kind. PC02's Capability.evidence is a single field on the kind, with no per-member slot.

## Findings

In PC02: (1) make evidence a TUPLE on the Capability (evidence: tuple[CapabilityEvidence,...]) so a mechanism can carry several distinct evidence claims, each with its own constraints/limitations/tier — matching today's per-fact granularity; (2) for list-cardinality kinds, evidence attaches to the list MEMBER (each host-extension entry has its own optional evidence), not the kind. Confirm the normalization-equivalence test covers a provider with two facts on one field and a provider with member-scoped host_capabilities evidence (gemini), else PC03 will silently drop the second/member-scoped facts and the equivalence gate won't catch it.

## Conclusion

Blocking-for-design: evidence must be multi-valued and member-addressable, or the merge is lossy for pi (two facts/field) and gemini (member-scoped fact). Fix the record shape in PC02 before the equivalence test is written, or the test will bake in the loss.
