# Evidence-only kinds and folded modifiers escape VAL-PCAP-009 — the enforcement has two holes it created

## Notes

The plan exists to close an ungoverned namespace, but the two design decisions just made re-open smaller ones:

1. Folded modifiers escape the catalogue. Decision 1 puts mcp-remote inside mcp_config.mechanism.capabilities:[remote] and cli-probe inside the cli-install mechanism. Because they are NOT catalogue kinds, VAL-PCAP-009 does not govern them — a typo ('remotte', 'prob_fn') is not caught by the catalogue, only by whatever (if anything) validates the ManagedConfigSpec.capabilities frozenset. We moved a small namespace out of enforcement rather than into it.

2. Evidence-only kinds are free-floating. model-vendor-injection etc. have mechanism_schema 'none' — there is no mechanism to co-locate their evidence WITH. Co-location was the whole justification for dropping the 'subject' field. Evidence-only kinds have nothing to attach to, so they risk becoming exactly the free-floating bucket capability_facts is today, just under a nicer name.

## Findings

Close both holes in PC01: (1) MODIFIERS — the mechanism_schema for 'managed-config-spec' must enumerate its legal modifier values (capabilities ∈ {remote, ...}) and validate the frozenset against that closed set (VAL-PCAP extension), so modifiers are governed even though they are not top-level kinds. Same for cli-install-recipe's inner fields. (2) EVIDENCE-ONLY KINDS — require a mandatory anchor: an evidence-only kind's evidence MUST carry an external: fact_anchor pointing at a real artifact (test path, evidence doc, tool version), and validation rejects an evidence-only kind with no anchor. That keeps them provenance-bearing records, not free-floating claims — preserving the anti-dumping-ground guarantee that motivated the whole plan.

## Conclusion

The two just-made decisions each carve a small ungoverned space out of the enforcement the plan is FOR. Governed-modifier value sets and mandatory anchors on evidence-only kinds close them; add both to PC01 scope or the merged model reproduces the original problem in miniature.
