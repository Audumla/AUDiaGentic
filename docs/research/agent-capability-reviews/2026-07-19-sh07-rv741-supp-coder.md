## Assessment

Strong structural execution for a supp-tier profile — the module shape, key
contract, and redaction discipline needed no repair. The failures were
evidence-grounding failures: it invented plausible enum values rather than
grepping the producer for the real ones. Two lessons, one each way:

- Prompt lesson (controller): when a projection consumes recorded evidence,
  the handoff must name the producer file and require the worker to derive the
  enum from it ("map the kinds recorded by `session.turn.<kind>` in
  agents_gateway_sessions.py; do not invent values").
- Capability lesson (worker): supp-coder will follow a spec literally,
  including its gaps. Treat unspecified vocabularies in supp handoffs as
  defects in the prompt, not things the worker will research on its own.
